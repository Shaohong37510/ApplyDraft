Frequently asked questions

Does ApplyDraft send emails automatically?
No. ApplyDraft only creates drafts in your Gmail or Outlook inbox. Nothing is sent until you open the draft and click send yourself. You review every email before it goes out.
What Gmail and Outlook permissions does ApplyDraft need?
For Gmail, ApplyDraft requests only the gmail.compose scope — permission to create drafts only. We cannot read your existing emails. For Outlook, we request Mail.ReadWrite to create drafts. We never store your email credentials.
Can I use it for cold email outreach to companies not actively hiring?
Yes. ApplyDraft works great for cold outreach — you can add any firm's email address and generate a tailored application even if they don't have an active posting. Many users apply to firms speculatively this way.
How is the email subject line handled?
ApplyDraft checks whether a firm specifies a required subject line format in their job posting and follows it exactly. If no format is specified, it generates a clean, professional subject line automatically.

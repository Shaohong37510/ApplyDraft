Frequently Asked Questions

Does ApplyDraft send applications automatically without my review?
No. ApplyDraft only creates drafts in your Gmail or Outlook inbox. You review every email and cover letter before deciding to send — you are always in full control. No bot sends anything on your behalf.
Will my cover letters actually sound personalized, or will they be generic AI filler?
ApplyDraft analyzes the full job description — required skills, seniority, company background — before writing. Each cover letter is generated from scratch for that specific job and grounded in your actual resume and experience. It is not a template with a swapped company name.
Which email providers does ApplyDraft support?
ApplyDraft connects to Gmail (via Google OAuth) and Outlook (via Microsoft OAuth). After connecting, generated application emails are saved directly as drafts in your inbox — ready for your review.
How many jobs can I apply to with ApplyDraft?
There is no hard limit. Each application costs approximately 1 credit. With the Standard pack (100 credits, $69), you can generate 100 fully personalized applications.
Do I need a subscription?
No subscription required. ApplyDraft uses a pay-per-use credit system. Buy credits once — they never expire. No recurring charges.
How is ApplyDraft different from LazyApply or Simplify?
LazyApply auto-fills forms in bulk but does not generate personalized cover letters and has a known history of errors. Simplify autofills forms faster but does not write cover letters or application emails. ApplyDraft generates a truly personalized cover letter PDF and email body for each job — and only saves drafts, never sends without your review.
Will employers know I used AI to write my application?
ApplyDraft generates applications based on your own resume, writing style, and examples you provide. The output is tailored to each job and reflects your real background. You can also review and edit every draft before sending.
Can I use my own Claude API key?
Yes. You can connect your own Anthropic Claude API key in Settings. This gives you full control over AI usage and costs without consuming ApplyDraft credits.

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal and ethical to use?
Yes. ApplyDraft is an AI writing assistant — it helps you write personalized applications faster, the same way Grammarly helps you write emails better. You review every application before sending. No deception, no misrepresentation.
Will employers know I used AI?
ApplyDraft generates applications based on your actual resume, experience, and writing examples you provide. The output is grounded in your real background and tailored to each job. You also review and can edit every draft — the final application is genuinely yours.
How many applications can I generate?
There is no hard limit. Each application costs approximately 1 credit. You can generate 10 or 100+ in a single session. Many users apply to 50–100 positions in one afternoon.
What's the difference between ApplyDraft and LazyApply?
LazyApply auto-fills job application forms in bulk, often with errors, and does not generate personalized cover letters — every application gets the same generic content. ApplyDraft focuses on email-based applications with a unique, tailored cover letter and email for each job. You also review every draft before it's sent — LazyApply submits automatically without your review.
